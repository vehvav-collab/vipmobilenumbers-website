Business Mobile Numbers: How to Choose a Memorable Number

Practical considerations for businesses choosing a memorable mobile number for customers, teams and marketing.

Updated for the current website structure. Availability, pricing and operator conditions should always be confirmed directly before a transaction.

Prioritise recall

A business number often appears on websites, signage, invoices, social media and advertisements. A structure that is easy to read aloud can reduce mistakes.

Choose a simple pattern

Repeated endings, pairs and short sequences can be easier to communicate than an irregular pattern. The right choice depends on the business and audience.

Keep brand consistency

If your business already uses a repeated number theme or memorable digits, consider a pattern that is consistent with your existing communication materials.

For teams and retailers

If multiple people will share the number, test how easily staff can read it back over a phone call.
