Tetra numbers

“Tetra” is commonly used in number-selling terminology for a four-of-a-kind repetition within a mobile number, such as a block of four identical digits.

Penta numbers

“Penta” commonly refers to a five-digit repetition or a pattern containing five of the same digit, depending on the seller’s classification.

Why people choose them

Strong repetition can make a number visually distinctive and easier to recall. Businesses may use memorable patterns when they want a number that customers can repeat easily.
